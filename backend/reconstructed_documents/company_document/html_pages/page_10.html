(II)
Inter-office
adjustments (net)
The inter-office adjustments balance, if in
credit, shall be shown under this head. The
bank should first segregate the credit
entries outstanding for more than 5 years in
the inter-branch account and transfer them
to a separate Blocked Account which
should be shown under ‘Other Liabilities
and Provisions - Others’. While arriving at
the net amount of inter-branch transactions
for inclusion here, or Schedule 11, as the
case may be, the aggregate amount of
Blocked Account should be excluded and
only the amount representing the remaining
credit entries should be netted against debit
entries. Only net position of inter-office
accounts, inland as well as foreign, shall be
shown here.
