(vii) Disclosure on Co-Lending Arrangements (CLA):
(a) In addition to the applicable disclosure requirements under extant
regulations, a bank shall also prominently disclose on their website, a list of
all active CLA partners.
(b) A bank shall also make appropriate disclosures in their financial
statements, under ‘Notes to Accounts’, relating to necessary details of CLAs
on an aggregate basis. The details may inter alia include quantum of CLAs,
weighted average rate of interest, fees charged / paid, broad sectors in which
