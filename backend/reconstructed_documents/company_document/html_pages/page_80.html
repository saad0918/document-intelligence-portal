(12) Disclosure of penalties imposed by the RBI
(i)
Penalties imposed by the RBI under the provisions of the (a) Banking
Regulation Act, 1949, (b) Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, and
(c) Government Securities Act, 2006 (for bouncing of SGL) shall be disclosed
in the ‘Notes to Accounts’ to the balance sheet in the concerned bank’s next
Annual Report.
(ii)
In the case of a foreign bank, the penalty shall be disclosed in the ‘Notes to
Accounts’ to the next balance sheet for its Indian operations. A bank shall
make appropriate disclosures on the nature of the breach, number of
instances of default and the quantum of penalty imposed.
