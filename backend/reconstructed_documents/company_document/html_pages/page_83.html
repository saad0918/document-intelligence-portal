(ii)
Bancassurance business: The details of fees / brokerage earned in respect
of insurance broking, agency and bancassurance business undertaken by a
bank shall be disclosed for both the current year and previous year.
(iii) Marketing and distribution: A bank shall disclose the details of fees /
remuneration received in respect of the marketing and distribution function
(excluding bancassurance business) undertaken by it.
