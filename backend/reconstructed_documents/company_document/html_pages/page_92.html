E.
Unreconciled balances
27. Unreconciled credit balances in any transitory account representing unclaimed
balances shall not be transferred to the profit and loss account or to any reserves.
F.
Deferred tax liability (DTL) on Special Reserve created under Section 36(1)
(viii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961
28. A bank shall make provisions for DTL on the Special Reserve created under
Section 36(1)(viii) of Income Tax Act, 1961.
G.
Window dressing
29. A bank shall ensure that balance sheet and profit and loss account reflect true and
fair picture of its financial position.
30. Instances of window dressing of financials, short provisioning, misclassification of
NPAs, under-reporting / incorrect computation of exposure / risk weight, incorrect
capitalisation of expenses, capitalisation of interest on NPAs, deliberate inflation
of asset and liabilities at the end of the financial year and subsequent reversal
immediately in next financial year, etc. shall be viewed seriously and appropriate
penal action in terms of the provisions of the BR Act shall be considered.
